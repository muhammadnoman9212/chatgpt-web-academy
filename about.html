About Almadni Quranic Academy

Almadni Quranic Academy is an online Islamic learning platform dedicated to teaching the Holy Quran to students of all ages, including children, adults, and elderly learners.

Our academy focuses on correct Quran recitation, Tajweed rules, Islamic values, and character building. We provide a respectful and comfortable learning environment suitable for beginners as well as advanced students.

With qualified male and female teachers, one-to-one online classes, and flexible schedules, we ensure that every student receives personalized attention and quality education.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread Quranic knowledge worldwide using modern online teaching methods while maintaining Islamic principles, sincerity, and excellence in education.
